# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — First Emergence (2026-08-06)

The founding release of SALTWATER: an agentic orchestration harness
transcribed from the developmental biology of *Crocodylus porosus*.

### What's in the clutch

- **The five stages, working and tested** — `Nest` (environment-as-womb),
  `Vocalize` (quorum-gated emergence), `Transport.jaws()` (guarded migration,
  `bite_pressure=0.0` enforced), `Estuary` (live staging with fences that
  fade by demonstrated competence), `OpenWater` (wave-based graduated
  abandonment, release bar 0.8)
- **Zero dependencies**, Python 3.11+, asyncio-native, ~600 lines
- **Connect-anything hooks** — `agent.ready()` for consensus, an `exercise()`
  for training, a thrive score for release; identical for local (`sovereign`)
  and cloud (`pelagic`) agents, `estuarine` hybrid by default
- **`examples/simple_agent.py`** — a full three-agent lifecycle, mound to ocean
- **`examples/clutch_of_scrapers.py`** — the real-life application: a clutch of
  research agents raised against live HTTP services (`/health`, `/canary`),
  nest to open water, in ~40 lines
- **12-test suite** — `python -m unittest discover tests -v`
- **Original artwork** — banner plus four stage paintings hung through the README
- **MIT licensed** — the protocol is two hundred million years old and belongs
  to everyone

> The mother withdraws. That was the point.
